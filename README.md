A front page for Friday 20 November 2026, made after the day had ended in every time zone. It shows 50 attributed stories from six world regions: four leads, twelve illustrated cards and 34 short items. It also has a timeline of the day from east to west, a locator map, and a date check. The date check names the stories left out because they broke on Thursday 19 or Saturday 21 November (checked against Wikipedia's Current events pages for those days). This complete version replaces the early 24- and 28-story pages. It also removes two Thursday stories that the early pages showed as Friday news. The facts come from public reports (AP, BBC, DW, France 24, Guardian, NPR, CNBC, Reuters, Al Jazeera, Africanews, The Hindu, UN News, Deccan Herald, Daily Trust, ABC) and Wikipedia, written in our own words. Every picture and the map are drawn in code (map outlines: Natural Earth, public domain). `main.py` checks `entry.png` against the contest's limits and copies it to the output folder.
